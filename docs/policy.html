Fluffy Climbers: Tower Jump Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-27

In short

  • Fluffy Climbers: Tower Jump ("the Game") has no account system of its own, no server of its own except an optional service (the weekly leaderboard, section 4a, and the save backup copy, section 4) that runs only if you sign in with Google Play Games, no analytics or crash-reporting software of its own (the advertising and purchasing libraries report their own diagnostics to Google and Unity, sections 5 to 7), no chat and no push notifications.
  • Your progress (levels, stars, coins, gems, heroes, power-ups, records and settings) is stored on your device. If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, a copy of your progress is also stored in your Google account through Google's Saved Games service, and a second backup copy of the whole save is stored on Google Cloud servers (Firebase) in Mumbai, India, linked to your Firebase user id, so that it can be restored on another device if Saved Games cannot supply it. Guests keep their progress on the device only.
  • If you sign in with Google Play Games and play the optional weekly leaderboards, your Google Play Games display name, a link to your Google Play Games profile picture, and your weekly scores are stored on Google Cloud servers in Mumbai, India (Firebase), and other players can see your display name, picture and score on the boards. Guests are never on a leaderboard (section 4a).
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play (and, where the Game is offered there, the Apple App Store). We never receive card or bank details.
  • The Game shows optional rewarded video ads, only after you tap an ad button, and full-screen interstitial ads when you restart or leave a run (never within two minutes of the previous one; the exact rule is in section 6). A one-time purchase, Remove Ads, stops ads from being shown. The advertising software built into the Game (the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which serves Google AdMob ads) starts with the Game and loads the next ad in advance, so from the first launch it uses your device's advertising ID and shares device, app and ad-interaction data with Google, whether or not you watch an ad. Where the law requires it, Google's consent form (the User Messaging Platform) asks for your choices before ads are requested, and you can change them at any time under "Privacy choices" in Settings.
  • You can play as a guest, unlink your Google account, reset your progress (which also deletes your weekly leaderboard scores; what you bought in the shop and still have is kept), change your ad choices in Settings, and reset or delete your advertising ID at any time.
  • The Game is not directed to children under 13. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to this policy and to our Terms of Use for you. See section 15.

1. Who we are

Fluffy Climbers: Tower Jump ("the Game", "we", "us", "our") is a 2D vertical-jumping arcade game for mobile devices published by J. P. Dash, an individual developer based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. The Game is distributed on Google Play under the application id com.jdlabs.fluffyclimbers and may later be distributed on the Apple App Store.

For privacy questions, requests and complaints, contact jpdash.creator@gmail.com. J. P. Dash is the controller (under the GDPR and the UK GDPR), the business (under the CCPA/CPRA), and the data fiduciary and grievance officer (under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023) for the limited personal data described in this policy.

2. Scope and how to read this policy

This policy explains what data the Game handles, what stays on your device, what third parties receive through the software built into the Game, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to the Game on every platform where we offer it, to its testing and pre-release builds, and to e-mail you send us. It does not cover the services of Google (including AdMob), Apple or Unity; their own policies are linked where they apply. Your use of the Game is governed by our Terms of Use, published next to this policy. Both documents can be opened at any time from the Game's Settings screen (the Privacy and Terms buttons) and from the store listing.

Where this policy says "we receive" or "we collect", it means that data reaches the developer. Most of the data described below is processed only on your device, or only by the third-party service named, and never reaches us. We do not operate a server for normal play. The only exceptions are the optional weekly leaderboard (section 4a) and the backup copy of your save (section 4), both used only when you are signed in; they run on Google Cloud services that we configure and control and that keep a small database of the players who use them.

3. Data stored on your device

The Game keeps the following in its private app storage on your device:

  • Progress: levels cleared, stars earned, best scores, clear counts, timed records and medals per level and per gameplay mode, best floor and records in endless and time-attack modes, unlocked regions, and which hero introductions you have seen.
  • Wallet and items: coins, gems, owned power-ups, owned heroes and their copies, chest-opening counter, bundle offers and their timers, reward claims and daily or weekly counters, and subscription claim state (days left, last claim day).
  • Purchase records (purchase ledger): for each real-money purchase, the product id, the kind of product (gem pack, subscription or Remove Ads), the store transaction id and the time, plus a record of what you bought in the shop and still have (coins, gems, power-ups and hero copies) and whether you own Remove Ads. These records let the Game restore your purchases, keep what you bought when you reset your progress, and prevent a purchase or a one-time bonus from being granted twice. They contain no payment details. While you are signed in with Google Play Games, they are also part of your cloud save and of the backup copy (section 4), so your purchases can be restored after you reinstall the Game.
  • Account fields: whether you chose guest or Google play, the Google Play Games player id of a linked account, the time of the last cloud sync, the time of the last change, and when the "connect Google" reminder was last shown.
  • Settings: sound, music, menu music, haptics, reduced motion, left-handed layout, text size, gameplay difficulty, pace ghost, and promotional flags. Development-only test switches exist in development builds and have no effect in release builds.
  • Other: the hero you have chosen, which first-time help screens you have seen and, if you use the weekly leaderboards (section 4a), your leaderboard choice, your own display name, runs waiting to be sent and prizes already received.

This data is written as plain (unencrypted) files in the app's private folder, which other apps cannot read on an unmodified device. Release (store) builds of the Game use Android's default backup setting, which is on: Android may therefore copy the save into your Google device backup (a Google service that we do not control and cannot read) and restore it when you reinstall the Game or set up a new device, and may include it in a device-to-device transfer. You can switch device backup off in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Backup). Pre-release development builds that we use for testing switch Android backup off. Apart from such a backup, the only off-device copies are the optional cloud save and the backup copy described in section 4. Nothing in this section is sent to us unless you sign in (the backup copy, section 4, and the weekly leaderboards, section 4a). Uninstalling the Game deletes the files on your device; a Google device backup or cloud save, if one exists, is not deleted by uninstalling.

4. Google Play Games sign-in and cloud save (optional)

On first launch you choose Sign in with Google Play or Play as guest. You can change your choice later in Settings. Signing in is never required to play.

Automatic sign-in check. On Android, the Google Play Games Services software starts with the Game and performs Google's automatic sign-in check at each launch, also if you chose guest play. Depending on your own Play Games settings, Google may then sign you in to Play Games, and it processes the related device and account data under its own policy. As a guest, the Game does not save your player id or name, uploads no save and sends nothing to our server.

What the Game accesses. When you sign in, the Game uses the Google Play Games Services software on your device and receives two things from it: your Play Games player id (an identifier Google creates for you for this Game only) and your Play Games display name. The display name is shown only to you, in the Settings screen of your own device, unless you use the weekly leaderboards (section 4a). The player id is stored in your save so that the Game can recognise the linked account. The Game does not receive your Google e-mail address, real name, contacts, friends list or photo files. (If you use the weekly leaderboards, section 4a, the Game also reads the link to your Play Games profile picture.)

Cloud save. While you are signed in, the Game stores one save file (the data listed in section 3, as plain JSON, which includes your settings (sound, music, haptics, reduced motion, left-handed layout, text size, gameplay difficulty, pace ghost, menu music) and the hero you have chosen, but not development-only test switches, together with a short summary and a time stamp) in Google's Saved Games service, which keeps it in the hidden app-data area of your Google Drive. Google processes that file under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Games Services terms. We have no access to that file and cannot read your cloud save; only the Game running on a device signed in to your Google account reads and writes it. The Game uploads a few seconds after a change (minor background changes such as play time at most once a minute) and when you pause or close the app. Your settings and chosen hero are applied to a new device only when you choose to use the cloud save there. When your device save and your cloud save differ, the Game shows you both and lets you choose; the one you do not pick is replaced.

Backup copy of your save (Firebase). While you are signed in with Google Play Games, the Game also stores a second, full copy of the same save file (the data listed above, compressed) on our own service, which runs on Google Cloud Run and keeps it in Cloud Firestore, in Google's data centre region in Mumbai, India (asia-south1), as one record that is identified by your Firebase user id (the id that Firebase Authentication creates when it signs in your Google Play Games account; see section 4a for what Firebase Authentication receives). The purpose is to let you restore your progress on another or a new device when Google Saved Games has no save or cannot be read: the Game tries Saved Games first and uses the backup copy only when Saved Games cannot supply a save, and when both exist and differ it shows you both and lets you choose. The copy is written when you tap Save Progress in Settings and, at most every five minutes, together with a normal cloud upload; it is replaced each time (we keep one copy, not a history). The request carries your Firebase sign-in token, the save and the usual network information (IP address, request logs, section 13). We do not read or use the content of the copy for anything else (not for scores, rewards, purchases, advertising or analysis), and no one else can see it: other players never see it and it is not part of any leaderboard. It is kept while your account is active and is deleted automatically about 24 months after it was last written, when you ask us to delete it (section 13). If you use Reset progress while signed in, the Game replaces it with the reset save at once; if you are offline or that fails, it is replaced the next time the Game writes the backup copy (to have the copy removed, e-mail us). The Game is not directed to children under 13 (section 15) and we do not knowingly store this copy for them.

Google's own features. Google Play Games may create a gamer profile for you and offer its own features (achievements, friends, profile visibility). Those features, and what other people can see of your Play Games profile, are controlled in the Google Play Games app and your Google account settings, not by us. Apart from the optional weekly leaderboard (section 4a), the Game publishes nothing about you.

Unlinking. "Unlink Google Account" in Settings makes the Game stop using the account and stops cloud save and backup copy uploads (for the weekly leaderboards see section 4a). Your progress stays on the device. The cloud save remains in your Google account until you delete it (Google Play Games settings > Delete Play Games data for this Game, or Google Drive > Settings > Manage apps > hidden app data), or until you use Reset progress while signed in, which replaces it with the reset save (progress wiped; your settings, sign-in choice, leaderboard name and what you bought are kept, section 3). The Firebase backup copy (see "Backup copy of your save") also stays after unlinking until it expires, you use Reset progress while signed in, or you ask us to delete it by e-mail (section 13).

Google Play Games sign-in and cloud save are available on Android. If we offer a comparable feature on another platform, we will describe it here first.

4a. Weekly leaderboard (optional, only when signed in)

The Game has three weekly leaderboards: Overall, Regions (the campaign levels) and Endless (which includes Time Attack). They rank the crown points you earn in a week. A week runs from Monday 00:00 to the next Monday 00:00 UTC. The leaderboards need a Google Play Games sign-in; a guest is never on a leaderboard and nothing in this section applies to a guest.

What is sent and stored. When you sign in and finish a run, the Game sends the run to our leaderboard service so that it can check the score: the level or tower and gameplay setting, the hero you used and its stat variant, the power-ups you used, the recorded inputs of the run (button presses by game tick), the score you reached, the game version and a random run id. The service signs you in to Firebase Authentication with your Google Play Games account, which gives it an anonymous-looking Firebase user id, and keeps for each week: your best crown score in each level and gameplay setting, for Endless and Time Attack up to your three best runs of the week in each region and gameplay setting (their crown scores are added together), your weekly totals, the crown calculation behind them that your phone uploads (for each level or tower and setting: floors reached, combo chains, the resulting crown and the time of each version; it is stored only so that the "your crowns" view shows the same numbers on all your devices and after a reinstall, and it is never used to rank players, set limits or give prizes), your display name (copied from Play Games, or the name you chose yourself, see "Your own display name" below) and the link to your Google Play Games profile picture (copied from Play Games), a short public player id that is derived from your Firebase user id so that boards do not show it, the time of your latest submissions, and any prize you claimed. Your display name and Google Play Games profile picture are shown to every other player on the boards (the top 100 of each board for the week). Your own rank is shown to you. We do not receive your e-mail address, real name, contacts, location or advertising ID through this feature.

Your own display name. Once you are signed in with Google, Settings lets you choose a display name for the leaderboards instead of your Google Play Games name (3 to 16 letters, numbers, spaces or underscores; a built-in filter refuses rude words). The name is your own choice: do not use your real name or any personal detail if you do not want other players to see it. It is saved in your game save (and in your cloud save) and, once you have confirmed the leaderboards, sent with your other leaderboard data and shown to other players. It is not checked for uniqueness. You can change it at any time, and clearing it brings your Google Play Games name back. We may hide or change a name that is offensive or impersonates someone.

Signing in joins you. Signing in with Google Play Games is your choice to take part. From then on your leaderboard name (the display name you chose, or your Google Play Games name) and profile picture are shown to other players, you appear on every board that still has room (with 0 points until you score), and your runs are sent to our server for checking. Before you sign in (first-launch screen, Account window, Settings or Leaderboard screen) the Game shows a notice that says this, with a link to this policy, and starts the sign-in only after you tap "Agree and sign in". Nothing is sent while you play as a guest, and you can leave the leaderboards at any time by switching to guest play in Settings (then close and reopen the Game, so that no further run is sent). The Game also asks our server for a one-time ticket with a random level seed before a run you want to submit; the ticket contains your Firebase user id, the level or tower, the setting and an expiry, and is stored in used form for a few days to stop it being reused.

Who processes it. The data is processed for us by Google as our processor, using Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Google Cloud Run, in Google's data centre region in Mumbai, India (asia-south1). Google's terms and privacy information for these services apply (the Google Cloud and Firebase privacy documentation).

How long it is kept. Weekly rows (your slot scores, your weekly totals, your uploaded crown calculation and the weekly boards) are deleted automatically 90 days after the week ends. Prize claim records are deleted on the same schedule. Your profile row (display name, picture link, public player id, the hero variants first used, and rate-limit counters) is kept while you use the leaderboard and until you ask us to delete it.

Your choices and deletion. Unlinking your Google account in the Game, or choosing to play as a guest, stops new submissions. It does not delete what is already stored. To have your leaderboard data deleted at once (profile, weekly rows and claims), e-mail jpdash.creator@gmail.com from the address you want us to reply to and tell us your Google Play Games player name; we delete it within one month and your name and picture disappear from the boards when the next board refresh runs. Reset progress in Settings also deletes your weekly leaderboard data: when you confirm a reset while signed in, the Game asks our leaderboard service to delete your weekly rows in every week (slot scores, weekly totals and your uploaded crown calculation), and your scores leave the boards of the open week. If you stay signed in, your profile is registered again straight away, so your name and picture can appear on the open week's boards with 0 points; to stay off the boards, switch to guest play and ask us to delete your data. A reset does not delete your profile row (display name, picture link, public player id, hero variants), your prize claim records or boards of weeks that already ended; those stay until you ask us to delete them by e-mail (above) or expire automatically after 90 days (profile: until you ask). If you are offline at that moment, or the request fails, the Game keeps the deletion request on your device and repeats it automatically (at the next sign-in and then regularly) until our service has confirmed it, and it sends no new scores until then; you do not need to do anything. Deleting the Game's Play Games data in your Google account does not by itself delete the leaderboard rows.

Prizes. The top players of a finished week can claim a prize (section 5 of the Terms of Use). Claiming one stores a record that you claimed it, so that it cannot be claimed twice.

5. Purchases and payments

The Game sells gem packs, four subscription plans (two weekly and two monthly plans) and a one-time Remove Ads purchase through the store's in-app purchase system: Google Play Billing on Android (through the Unity In-App Purchasing library on your device) and, where the Game is offered there, Apple's in-app purchase system.

  • Payment data. Payment is processed entirely by Google or Apple. We never receive card, bank, billing-address or payment-account details. Subscriptions renew automatically through Google Play until you cancel them in the Play Store (see the Terms of Use, section 7).
  • What the Game receives on your device. For a completed purchase the store software gives the Game the product id, the store order or transaction id, and a purchase token or receipt that the Game uses to verify the purchase before it grants the gems or subscription. The Game stores the product id, kind, transaction id and time in your save (section 3). The receipt itself is not kept by the Game beyond the purchase. The Game also reads the store's localised price text so that prices can be shown in your currency.
  • Restoring purchases. "Restore" in Settings asks the store software on your device for your purchase history for this Game and re-grants active subscription days and Remove Ads; the Game also runs this check by itself each time it starts. This reads data from your store account on your device; it is not sent to us.
  • What we receive from the store. Google Play and Apple provide developers with sales and subscription reports and order details (for example order id, country, amount, currency, tax and refund status) under their own policies. We use these reports only for accounting, tax, refund handling and fraud prevention, and keep them as long as tax and accounting laws require. We cannot identify you from them unless you contact us about an order.
  • Unity. The Unity In-App Purchasing library is made by Unity Technologies and runs the purchase flow on your device. It starts when the Game starts, also for guests and also if you never open the shop: it connects to the store, loads the product list and checks for unfinished purchases, an active subscription and Remove Ads. Once started, the library sends to Unity's purchasing service: the product, price and transaction details the store returns (product id, order id, purchase token), a Unity installation identifier and session identifier, device information (model, operating system version, language and locale), its own diagnostic, performance and crash data, and the country derived from your IP address. Unity uses this to run the purchasing service, to de-duplicate transactions and to give us sales reports; it keeps transaction data as long as the service needs it and its service logs for 30 days, and acts as our processor under the Unity Privacy Policy. Unity never receives payment details.

Purchases, refunds and subscription management are governed by the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy, or by Apple's media services terms and the Apple Privacy Policy.

6. Advertising

The Game is free to download and is partly funded by rewarded video ads and interstitial ads. A rewarded ad plays only after you tap an ad button (to revive, to double the coins of a level, or to get the daily free power-up), and you can always decline. An interstitial is a full-screen ad shown when you restart or leave a run: when you choose Restart, Shop or Home in the pause menu (including the pause after a revive) the Game tries to show one every time; after a failed or finished run (for example Retry, Next, Shop or Home) it tries on every second such action. In both cases never within two minutes of the previous interstitial, and only when an ad is already loaded (otherwise you go on at once). There are no banner ads. The one-time purchase Remove Ads switches off all interstitials and gives every ad reward at once without an ad; the advertising software itself still starts and loads ads in the background, as described below.

The ad software itself, however, starts together with the Game: once your consent choices are known (see "Consent and your ad choices" below) it initialises and loads the next rewarded ad and interstitial ad in advance, so that the ad is ready when you ask for it. The data listed below is therefore sent from the first launch of each play session, whether or not you ever watch an ad. Where the law requires consent, nothing is requested until you have answered the consent form.

Who serves the ads. Ads are delivered by Google AdMob, an advertising service of Google LLC, through the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the Game. Google acts as an independent controller for the data it handles when it serves and measures ads. If we add further ad networks we will list them here before they go live. The Game is not directed to children and its ad content rating is set to "T" (Teen), so AdMob is asked to show ads suitable for that rating.

Data the ad software processes. To request, show and measure an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK on your device and Google's advertising systems may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID on Android; the Game declares the AD_ID permission for this purpose), and, where applicable, other device or app identifiers;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location (country or city level) can be derived;
  • device and app information: device model, operating system and version, language, screen size, network type, app id and version, and SDK version;
  • ad interaction data: which placement requested an ad, impressions, clicks, video completion, and the reward signal that tells the Game you watched the ad to the end;
  • diagnostic and performance data about ad loading and display errors.

This data is used to deliver ads, to limit how often you see the same ad, to detect fraud and invalid traffic, to measure performance, to pay the Game for completed views and, where you have consented or the law allows it, to personalise the ads you see.

Consent and your ad choices. Where the law requires consent before ads are requested or personalised (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), or requires a privacy message (for example in some US states), the Game shows Google's consent form (the Google User Messaging Platform) before the first ad and passes your answer to the Google Mobile Ads SDK. You can review and change your answer at any time under Privacy choices in the Game's Settings, where that entry is available to you. If you decline personalised ads, Google may still use the advertising ID and device data for frequency capping, fraud prevention and measurement, and you will see non-personalised ads. On Android you can also reset your advertising ID, delete it, or opt out of ads personalisation at any time (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads); if you delete it, the ad software receives a string of zeros instead.

Ad content. The ads themselves come from third-party advertisers. Tapping an ad can open a third-party website or store page outside the Game, where that party's privacy policy applies.

The processing by Google is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. AdMob's terms are at admob.google.com/home/terms.

8. Data we do not collect

  • No account, sign-in, name, e-mail address, phone number or password is required or collected by the Game. Google sign-in is optional and handled by Google. If you use the weekly leaderboards (section 4a) the leaderboard service receives your display name (your Play Games name, or the name you chose yourself) and profile picture link, but never your e-mail address, real name or password.
  • No analytics, usage-tracking or crash-reporting software of ours is switched on (the Google Analytics for Firebase library is present but deactivated, section 7), and the Game sends no usage events to us. The only diagnostics that leave your device are those the Google Mobile Ads SDK, Google Play services and the Unity In-App Purchasing library report to their own makers (sections 5 to 7).
  • No precise location, contacts, calendar, photos, files, camera or microphone access.
  • No chat, messages or friend lists, and no user-generated content apart from the optional leaderboard display name. The only thing other players can see is your display name (your Play Games name, or the name you chose in Settings), profile picture and weekly score on the leaderboards, and only if you sign in and play them (section 4a).
  • No push or remote notifications; the Game has no notification feature at all.
  • No server of ours for normal play: the Game does not connect to a computer operated by us, except to our service for the weekly leaderboard (section 4a) and the save backup copy (section 4) when you are signed in. All other network activity is performed by the Google (including AdMob), Apple and Unity software described above.
  • No sale of personal data, and no cross-app tracking by us.

The weekly leaderboards (section 4a) were added on 2026-11-12 and the backup copy of your save (section 4) on 2026-11-26. If the Game gains another feature that changes any of this (for example events, notifications or analytics), we will update this policy before the feature is released and tell you about it in the Game or in the store release notes.

17. Security

Your progress is protected by your device's own security (app sandboxing, lock screen and device encryption). The Game's network traffic is performed by Google and Unity software over connections those providers secure, and by the Game itself to our service over HTTPS. Apart from the leaderboard data of section 4a and the backup copy of your save (section 4) we hold no player data on servers.
