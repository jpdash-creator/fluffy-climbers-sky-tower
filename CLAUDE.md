# Fluffy Climbers: Tower Jump - project guide for Claude

2D vertical-jumper for Android + iOS. Unity 6 LTS (C#), 2D URP. Data-driven: regions, levels, items and offers are JSON plus asset bundles, never hardcoded in scenes.

## CRITICAL REQUIREMENTS (owner, 2026-10-19): seamless gameplay, no improper overlap, accessibility
User experience and accessibility are critical. Gameplay must be seamless and never choppy; screen items must never overlap improperly. The full mandatory rule set (CUX-1 to CUX-10) is in `.claude/rules/ux-gameplay-critical.md` (always loaded) and `docs/11-critical-ux-gameplay-requirements.md`. Headlines: 60 fps with fixed-step sim + interpolation, zero per-frame allocations, instant input, no overlapping or clipped UI on any aspect, 48 dp touch targets, readable contrast, no colour-only cues (except platform kinds: owner decision 2026-11-01, no code-drawn cues on platforms, see CUX-7), reduced motion/haptics/left-handed options, clean sliced art. CUX tests (category `CriticalUX`) must stay green; a failing CUX test blocks a commit.

## Hard rules
- **OpenArt credits (MANDATORY GUARDRAIL):** never call a credit-consuming OpenArt tool (`openart_generate_image`, `openart_generate_video`, edits/upscales) without the owner's explicit chat approval for that exact batch. Ask first with full details: purpose, MCP and tool, model and settings, the full prompts, credits per job and total, balance before and after, alternatives. Full rule: `.claude/rules/openart-credit-guardrail.md`. Read-only OpenArt tools (account, model list/cost/form, creation list) are free to use.
- **Originality:** never mention, name or imitate any existing jumper game in code, art, prompts, store text or comments. Generic genre mechanics only.
- **Determinism:** the simulation and the level generator use fixed-point/integer math and the seeded RNG (xoshiro). No `float`, `UnityEngine.Random`, `Time.deltaTime` or `Physics` in `Simulation/` or generator code. Same seed gives the same level on every device and on the server.
- **Server authority:** currencies, purchases, chests and leaderboard scores are decided server-side. No secrets in the client.
- **Region heroes (owner 2026-11-01, docs 21 and 26):** each region has one Common hero (R1 Momo always visible, R2 Capi the forest girl (the Sporeling was deleted 2026-11-01; old saves naming it fall back to Momo), R3 Otto the sea otter, R5 Bao the Shaolin monk, R4 candy hero pending). A hero is hidden until its region is unlocked AND its one-time "New hero joins you!" pop-up (MetaController.ShowHeroReveal, HeroRevealCard, background `Art/Ui/hero_reveal_bg` = owner-picked picture, card_yellow fallback) was confirmed; saved in `heroesRevealed` (additive). Use `CharacterCatalog.Owns/Available/Selected` (they respect this), `ProgressService.PendingHeroReveals/MarkHeroRevealed`. A new hero = one `CharacterDefs` row with its `Region` plus art. **Hero sheet** (owner 2026-11-01, doc 21): screen `Hero` (`HeroSheetScreen.cs`, `MetaController.ShowHeroSheet`) opens from the (i) button right of the shown hero on the Heroes screen (`HeroInfo`) and the Heroes tiles (the HOME screen has no (i) and no hero tap since 2026-11-02); sleek centred small typography, spare height shared out by `HeroSheetScreen.Plan`; plates in `Assets/Resources/Art/HeroSheet` (cut by `Tools/art_pipeline/process_hero_sheet.py`), stats from `CharacterStats.Sheet`.
- **Hero physics (owner 2026-11-07, rebuilt Final Change 2 2026-11-23, doc 44 section 13):** every hero (25 lines x 5 tiers = 125) has four physics numbers: launch in tenths (192 = 19.2), Speed, Control, Wall bounce. Momo Common is the Forgiveness Index anchor and the Common reference (19.2 / 9 / 29 / 77, `HeroIndex.MomoCommon`; launch floor 18.4). Every other hero of the 5 ACTIVE lines is a band over its own line's Common on ALL FOUR numbers (Uncommon +3 to +7 percent, Rare +6 to +10, Elite +10 to +14, Legendary +14 to +18; the other Commons -3 to +3 percent of Momo), half-up rounding, and its measured FI (mean over all 125 levels) lies in the tier window widened by 0.005 (Common and Uncommon 1.00, Rare and Elite 1.00 to 1.03, Legendary 1.00 to 1.05; level 15 within 0.02 more, no level more than 0.10 outside). Rows live in `Assets/_Game/Economy/HeroTable.cs`, GENERATED by `Tools/hero_table_gen.py` from `Tests/Simulation/hero_gen/heroes.csv`, which `Tools/hero_band_select.py` writes from the measured table `hero_gen/fi_cells.csv` (`HeroFiCellsTests`, `HERO_FI=1`): never hand-edit them. The 100 rows of the inactive lines are data only. Proof: `HeroBotAllLevelsTests` (`HERO_BOT=1`), `HeroVariantTests` (every row and variant passes the gate from the table), `HeroPenaltyFinalTableTests`.
- **Hero rolls (owner 2026-11-12, doc 44 section 11):** every upper-tier hero (Uncommon to Legendary) rolls its own stats from a pre-validated pool when first obtained (`Economy/HeroRolls.cs`; pool = the `HeroTable` row + `HeroVariants.cs`, GENERATED by `Tools/hero_table_gen.py` from `Tests/Simulation/hero_gen/variants.csv`, every entry passed the FI gate over all 125 levels). Common stays fixed. Never hand-edit `HeroVariants.cs`. **Since 2026-11-17 every COPY rolls its own** (`SaveData.HeroCopyRolls`, `ProgressService.HeroVariant(id, copy)`/`StatsOf(spec, copy)`; the run, hero sheet and leaderboard upload use the CHOSEN copy; merge removes the merged cards' rolls and rolls the new tier copy fresh; the ledger keeps one roll per bought copy). **Final Change 2 (owner 2026-11-23, docs/44 sections 13, 14):** powers per tier are Uncommon none, Rare ONE (Wide Platforms, Shield, Safety Net, Wall Stick, Glide Cape, Slow Time), Elite ONE (Safety Net, Double Jump, Glide Cape, Slow Time, Flight, Rocket), Legendary TWO of the Elite list, rolled per copy WITH the stats: a roll (variant) = `statIndex + StatPoolSize x comboIndex` (`HeroRolls.PowersFor/Rolled/Compose`, `TierRules.PowerChoices/PowerCombos`, `ProgressService.HeroPowers/RolledSpec`); run, hero sheet and server replay read the chosen copy's powers; save field `heroRollRules` = 2 re-rolls every owned copy once on load (`MigrateHeroRolls`, also after a cloud replace). The stat bands and FI windows are in the Hero physics bullet; the region penalty keeps every stat inside the lower tier's band except Uncommon speed (doc 44 section 13).
- **Cloud save completeness (owner Final Change 3, 2026-11-23, docs/35):** the whole `SaveData` is the Play Games Saved Games file; now it also holds the chosen hero/copy (`selectedHero`, `selectedCopy`, `CharacterCatalog` writes through `ProgressService.SetSelection`, PlayerPrefs only mirror and migration) and the player settings (`settings` = `SyncedSettings`, applied to a device ONLY when a cloud save is adopted, never on upload; dev flags stay local). `SaveSync.Decide` also compares `SaveSync.Fingerprint` (power-ups, stars, claims, purchases, ads removed, subscription, selection, roll counter) and the durable `cloudDirty` marker, so offline changes upload at the next launch. Quiet saves (`ProgressService.CommitQuiet/TouchQuiet`: play time, bundle timer, settings) raise `QuietChanged`; `QuietUploadGate` allows one such upload per 60 s. Data deletion runbook: `Planning/PLAYER-DATA-DELETION.md`. Tests `CloudSaveCompletenessTests`, `QuietUploadGateTests`.
- **Region penalty (owner Final Change 2, 2026-11-23, doc 44 section 12):** a non-Common hero away from its line's home region (`HeroRegionPenalty.HomeRegion`; levels use the level's region, Endless and Time Attack the region they start from) loses 5/3/7/8 points (Uncommon/Rare/Elite/Legendary) of its stat gain over the line's Common on all four physics numbers (round half up, floor at Common), Rare and Elite lose their power, a Legendary one power drawn once per run from the run seed. One pure integer function set `Economy/HeroRegionPenalty.cs`, used by `GameSession.BuildRunCfg(playRegion)`, `RunSession.ApplyHeroPowers` and the server `RunReplayer.Replay`; popups show `PenaltyUi` (icon swap point `Resources/Art/Ui/icon_penalty`, code glyph fallback) next to PLAY/START. Tests `HeroRegionPenaltyTests`, `RegionPenaltyReplayTests`, `RegionPenaltyUxTests`.
- **No gear (owner 2026-10-24 and 2026-11-01):** there are no outfits, boots, hats or accessories. Characters (docs 20, 21) and power-ups are the only items. Since 2026-11-12 everything counts on the boards: revived runs, power-ups, the player's own rolled hero stats (no neutral Ranked mode).
- **IDs are permanent:** content IDs (`region_021_dragon_peak`, `lvl_021_01`, `pw_flight`) never change once shipped.
- **No allocations** in gameplay loops. Pool platforms and particles.
- Behaviour is code (rare app updates); content is data + bundles (frequent). Only assets and data are downloaded, never code.
- **Legal documents (owner, 2026-11-09):** `POLICY.md` (privacy policy) and `TERMS.md` (terms of use) at the repo root are the public, store-facing documents; `Planning/LEGAL-HOSTING.md` has the hosting steps, the Play Console mapping and the release blockers they assume (UMP consent prompt, in-app policy links, chest odds in the (i) popup, receipt validation, admin code). Any change that touches data handling (new SDK, permission, analytics, server, notifications, ad network, product kind, cloud feature, age handling) updates both files and their "Last updated" date in the same change, before the build ships. The documents say: not directed to children under 13, publisher J. P. Dash (Bengaluru, India), contact jpdash.creator@gmail.com, ads non-personalised by default, no own server.

## Build versioning
Every build uses the version vMAJOR.MINOR.BUILD (since 2026-11-09; file `Tools/version.json` with `major`, `minor`, `build`; rules in `.claude/rules/versioning.md`; `python Tools/version.py show`). Bump MINOR before the build for a feature, content drop or rule change (`python Tools/version.py bump minor`); MAJOR only by owner decision. There is no HOTFIX part any more: a fix-only build just raises BUILD, which the build script (`AndroidBuild.Build()`) increments on every build and never resets. Name the installed version in every report and build commit ("Build v0.52.68: ...").

## Layout rules from emulator report 2 (2026-11-02)
- Touch targets are checked in dp on the real density: `UiKit.MinTouchRef` (48 * Screen.dpi / 160 px divided by the canvas scale, never below `MinTouchPx`; 144 reference px on 720x1280 at 320 dpi). Popup actions (`ToyKit.PopupAction` icons) and the popup X use it.
- Settings: header fixed, the cream card scrolls inside `SettingsViewport`; the rows, the dev flag rows (GAME_JUMP_TEST, SYSTEM_TEST, development builds only) and the Reset progress button are all children of the card with a gap. `GameSettings.ShowDevRowsForTests` shows the dev rows in editor tests.
- Level screen (owner 2026-11-22, Final Change 1; `MetaController.Gameplay.cs`): from the top wallet pills, progress pills (n/25 levels and n/75 stars of the selected setting), the five Gameplay settings, then the region name with the Endless button, then the level path. The settings row has no plates: each slot is an icon with its name under it; the selected one has a white ring (`GameplayRingPx` 164), a bigger icon (146, the others 116 and slightly faded) and a bold white name, never colour alone. A tap redraws stars, locks and pills (`RefreshLevelPath`). Tests: `LevelScreenHasGameplayRowAboveRegionName`, `SwitchingTheGameplaySettingRedrawsStarsLocksAndPills`.
- Level and Endless popups are content-sized with ONE close (the X in the frame); PLAY/START centred. The pause popup (RunHud.cs) is content-sized (title + one icon row, no empty middle), has no X (Resume is the single way back) and its buttons are at least `UiKit.MinTouchRef`.
- Rewards region names break only at spaces (`\n`, horizontal overflow); shop (`ShopChrome.ScrollEndPad`) and rewards lists have end padding so the last card scrolls fully above the bottom bar.
- Tests: `CriticalUxLayoutTests` (SettingsRowsAndResetButtonStayInsideThePanel, LevelPopupHasOneCloseAndNoEmptyMiddle, RewardsRegionNamesAndListEndAreSafe, TouchMinimumFollowsRealDensity).
- Heroes screen: `StageScene.CharacterLayout()` sizes the stage to 44 percent of the canvas height (700..1000 px, `Stage.Scale`), the dark `ListPanel` is a child of the full-screen stage root (no gap under the grass, whatever the notch), the roster is 3 tiles per row sized from the real safe width with a margin of `CharactersScreen.SideMargin` (28 px) and the (i) button sits in the bottom right corner of the stage. Helpers `UiKit.CanvasSizeRef/SafeSizeRef/TopInsetRef`.
