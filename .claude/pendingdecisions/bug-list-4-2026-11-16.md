## 3. Power-ups
- Double Jump, Wall Stick, Shield each last exactly 20 s. Hero powers use the matching normal power-up timing (no separate timing).
- Magnet is removed (no coin pickups). Replaced by three 1,000-coin power-ups (gem price stays the 50/75 gems ladder of the old Magnet):
  1. **Safety Net** (owner picked "armed for the whole run"): can be armed any time before a fall (or equipped at run start). Not timed. On the first fall it bounces the player automatically to a higher platform about 10 floors up. Once per run; sits as a small icon while armed; does not consume the revive offer.
  2. **Glide Cape** (proposed by Claude, owner asked for unique options; first four options were rejected): while the jump button is held in the air the hero falls slowly and steers further, 20 s.
  3. **Wide Platforms** (proposed): for 20 s every platform is 50 percent wider (landing zone), good for Hard and Insane.
  Ids: `pw_safety_net`, `pw_glide`, `pw_wide_platforms`; `pw_magnet` removed (old saves migrate: refund its stock as coins? decision: convert owned magnets to Safety Nets 1:1).
- Icons for the three: OpenArt generation only after the owner approves credits in chat (separate ask). Until then placeholder sprites.
- Shop, hero powers, chests, bundles, rewards, hero sheet text, gameplay: update everywhere the magnet was referenced.

## 4. Hero power (Uncommon to Legendary)
- The hero's own power is free ONCE per run; it uses the matching power-up and its 20 s timing (not a separate power).
- Free use resets on Retry / restart. A revive (ad or gems) does NOT reset it: if the free use was not used before the revive it can still be used; if it was already used it stays used.
- After the free use is spent the button shows the owned count of that power-up (0 if none bought) and works only from owned stock.
- Goal: nobody can clear levels faster by cheating with free powers.

## 5. Combos and power-ups
- Combo does not stack with power-ups. A power-up jump adds exactly +1 to the combo and then ENDS the chain (next normal jump starts a new chain at 1). Combos build only from normal gameplay jumps.

## 6. Crown formula (replaces combo chained sum)
- Crown = (floors + sum over every chain of chain_length^2) x 5 x (1 + setting bonus). All integer, round half up.
- Example: floor 80, chains 5, 10, 20: (80 + 25 + 100 + 400) x 5 x (1 + bonus). Owner example single chain 48 on Insane: (80 + 2304) x 5 x 1.5 = 17,880.
- Old crown (floors x 10 + sum of n^2 x 5 per step) is gone. Saved crowns without a stored run cannot be converted: reset all saved crowns and weekly crowns once (migration), medals/stars untouched.
- Pop-up text shows: floors, chain list squares summed, x5, setting bonus, crown. Server replay (Tools/leaderboard_service) and Simulation tests/golden replays follow. Redeploy of the service needs the owner's OK.
- Medals still depend on pace.
